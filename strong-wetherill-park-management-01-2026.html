EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Membership

The studio ended January 2026 with 245 total members comprising 184 active and 61 suspended memberships. The active rate improved to 75.1% in January, recovering from December's 65.8% seasonal low. Net membership declined by 8 members across the quarter, with January delivering 15 sales against 17 cancellations, achieving near-replacement performance.

Revenue

Quarterly gross revenue totalled $167,187 across November-January. January revenue reached $52,669, recovering from December's seasonal low of $49,981 but remaining below November's $64,537. Membership revenue declined steadily from $43,714 in November to $41,549 in January, representing 78.9% of January's gross revenue and signalling the need for focused acquisition to reverse this trend.

Utilisation

January utilisation reached 50.32% for booking and 47.34% for attendance, representing improvements from December's 46.65% and 43.39% respectively. Both metrics remain significantly below optimal targets of 75-85% for booking utilisation and 70-75% for attendance utilisation. The 2.98% gap between booking and attendance rates stays well within the 10% threshold, indicating healthy conversion from bookings to actual attendance.

Sales vs Cancellations

January delivered 15 membership sales against 17 cancellations, achieving near-replacement performance and preventing further membership base erosion. Intro offer sales surged to 69 units driven by the STRONG Starter Pass promotion, up from 15 in December. Pack sales declined from 81 units in November to 44 in January, demonstrating high seasonal sensitivity.

Key Issues

  • Declining membership revenue: Revenue decreased from $43,714 (Nov) to $41,549 (Jan), reflecting net loss of 8 members and requiring focused conversion activity to reverse the trend
  • Utilisation significantly below target: Booking utilisation at 50.32% sits 25-35 percentage points below the 75-85% target, while attendance utilisation at 47.34% is 23-28 points below the 70-75% target
  • Acquisition-cancellation imbalance: January's 15 sales vs 17 cancellations achieved only replacement-level performance, insufficient to drive membership growth and revenue recovery
  • Attrition above optimal: Rates fluctuated between 4.67% and 6.64% throughout the quarter, consistently above the exceptional 5% benchmark though below the sub-7% target

Active Interventions Being Deployed

  • STRONG Starter Pass conversion focus: 69 intro offers sold in January creating robust pipeline for membership conversions as promotion concludes in February
  • FOH restructure completion: New structure establishing seamless customer journey with ongoing staff training to optimise sales pipeline efficiency and conversion execution
  • Recovr retention software implementation: Supporting systematic retention efforts and pipeline management to address attrition and improve member lifecycle tracking
  • New intro offer transition: Mid-February launch of $50 for 5 days offer, which testing has shown increases conversion rates compared to previous offers
  • Operational systems refinement: Building routines around daily scheduled tasks and proficiency with software to enact processes per sales pipelines and schedules

Expected Outcomes

  • Convert January's 69 intro offer pipeline into memberships to reverse declining membership revenue trend from $41,549 toward November's $43,714 baseline
  • Improve booking utilisation from 50.32% toward 75-85% target range through increased member acquisition and class booking engagement initiatives
  • Increase attendance utilisation from 47.34% toward 70-75% optimal range via improved member engagement and attendance accountability systems
  • Reduce attrition from 6.64% toward the exceptional 5% benchmark through systematic retention initiatives and Recovr software utilisation

SUMMARY & PATH FORWARD

STRONG Pilates Wetherill Park concluded the November-January quarter with mixed performance indicators requiring focused operational attention. The studio maintained 245 total members with a 75.1% active rate in January, recovering from December's seasonal disruption but down 8 members from November's 253. Quarterly revenue of $167,187 demonstrated the studio's financial foundation, though declining membership revenue from $43,714 to $41,549 signals the need for sustained acquisition focus. Session utilisation improved to 50.32% booking and 47.34% attendance in January, though both metrics remain significantly below optimal targets of 75-85% and 70-75% respectively. The quarter's strongest positive signal came from January's 69 intro offer sales driven by the STRONG Starter Pass promotion, creating a conversion pipeline to address the membership revenue decline.

The studio's operational priorities centre on converting the intro offer pipeline into memberships while simultaneously improving capacity utilisation. With booking utilisation 25-35 percentage points below target and attendance utilisation 23-28 points below target, substantial growth potential exists without adding sessions. The FOH restructure completion and active staff training should support improved sales pipeline efficiency and conversion execution. Attrition rates fluctuating between 4.67% and 6.64% demonstrate performance below the sub-7% target, requiring continued focus on retention initiatives alongside acquisition efforts. The February transition to the $50 for 5 days intro offer, combined with converting the existing Starter Pass pipeline, positions the studio to rebuild membership revenue and drive utilisation improvements through increased member acquisition and engagement.

Key Takeaways

  • Quarter ended with 245 total members (down 8 from November) and $167,187 revenue, with membership revenue declining from $43,714 to $41,549 indicating acquisition urgency
  • Utilisation metrics improved to 50.32% booking and 47.34% attendance in January but remain 25-35 and 23-28 percentage points below optimal targets respectively, representing significant untapped capacity
